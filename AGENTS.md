# Project Instructions

## UI Principles

Always follow:

- **Hick's Law**: Minimize cognitive overload by reducing choices and complexity. Group options logically, provide smart defaults, and keep primary navigation straightforward.
- **Fitts's Law**: Optimize touch target size and proximity. Critical controls (such as playback buttons, primary navigation, and interactive icons) must be at least 48dp x 48dp and positioned within easy thumb reach.
- **Jakob's Law**: Align with established media streaming mental models (e.g., Spotify, YouTube Music). Use familiar iconography, persistent mini players, expandable full-screen sheets, and conventional search/filter patterns.
- **Miller's Law**: Chunk information into digestible groups of 5–9 items (7 ± 2). Structure content feeds with section carousels, categorized lists, and modular settings cards.
- **Gestalt Law of Proximity**: Group related interface elements closely with tight spacing (e.g., artwork, title, and artist within 2–4dp), while separating distinct sections and cards with generous negative space (16–24dp).
- **Aesthetic-Usability Effect**: Maintain a visually appealing, polished design. Utilize smooth glassmorphic surfaces, ambient glowing accents, consistent typography hierarchies, and subtle motion feedback to enhance perceived usability and delight.
